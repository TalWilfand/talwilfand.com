About Me

My name is Tal Wilfand.

I'm a game developer living in the greater Seattle area. I graduated from DigiPen Institute of Technology in 2024 with a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Game Design. One of my main Game Projects was One in A Krillion , available on Steam. I've been passionate about video games for as long as I can remember. As a developer, some of my focuses includes Technical Design, Gameplay Programming, and Game AI. Being able to contribute to an industry that has provided so much for me growing up is a dream come true. This portfolio showcases some of the projects I've worked on, the skills I've developed, and my journey in game development.
